# 🚨 YOUR BIG FREE-CAMPING RULE

This is extremely important.

Don't use Google Maps and think:

> "That looks like national forest."

Instead use the **Forest Service Motor Vehicle Use Map + current forest orders**.

For Coconino, the general rule is that dispersed camping is allowed in appropriate forest areas, but there are specific closures/designated areas. The forest generally has a 14-day limit, with additional restrictions in certain corridors. ([Forest Service][10])

Kaibab currently has a **14 days in 30 days** camping restriction, plus a major restriction against dispersed camping within **one mile of developed campgrounds/cabins on the Williams and Tusayan districts**. ([BurnDates][16])

That means:

### DON'T DO THIS

Park right next to:

**Dogtown Lake Campground**

and say:

> "I'm dispersed camping."

If you're inside the restricted radius, you're potentially violating the order.

Instead find a legal dispersed site **outside the restricted zone**.
