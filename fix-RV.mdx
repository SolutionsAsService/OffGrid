# 2. What needs to be fixed first

Based on what you've told me, I would divide the work into four categories.

### 🚨 Priority 1 — Power steering

You specifically need the **power steering belt installed/repaired**.

Before driving any significant distance, determine:

* Is the belt missing?
* Is it broken?
* Is the tensioner bad?
* Is the idler pulley bad?
* Is the power-steering pump seized?
* Is there a fluid leak?
* Is the belt routing correct?
* Is the crank pulley damaged?
* Does the steering pump turn freely?

**Do not just install a belt without finding out why the previous belt came off/broke.**

---

### 🚨 Priority 2 — Electrical

You previously had a **melted/blown fuse situation** involving the solar/battery/inverter system.

That needs a proper inspection.

Specifically ask for:

* Battery cables
* Battery terminals
* Main battery fuse
* Fuse holder
* Inverter wiring
* Ground cables
* Battery-bank configuration
* Solar-controller wiring
* 12-V fuse panel
* Shore-power system
* Converter/charger
* Alternator charging
* Any melted insulation
* Any overheated terminals
* Any undersized wiring

**Do not increase the fuse size to stop it from blowing.**

---

### 🚨 Priority 3 — Chassis/mechanical

While you're there, have them inspect:

* Brakes
* Steering
* Suspension
* Tires
* Wheel bearings where applicable
* Cooling system
* Belts

### Priority 4 — RV house systems

# 28. Especially Important: Your Electrical System

Tell the RV technician exactly what happened:

> "A fuse/connection previously overheated and melted while running a mini fridge, smart TV and evaporative cooler. I need the entire high-current DC system inspected rather than simply replacing the fuse."

That sentence matters.

You don't want:

**melted fuse → bigger fuse → melted wire → fire.**

You want:

**melted fuse → determine why → repair cable/termination/fuse holder → verify current draw → load-test → document.**
